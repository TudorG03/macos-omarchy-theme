First public release.

- Theme with colors, wallpapers, GTK styling and Hyprland animations, blur and shortcuts
- Menu bar, Dock, Spotlight, Launchpad, Mission Control, Control Center, App Switcher, hot corners and volume popup
- Working yellow and green traffic lights on Ghostty and Nautilus
