# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Traffic lights on every app: `install.sh` now builds hyprbars for browsers, web apps and other apps without their own
- Yellow and green now work in all GNOME and GTK apps, not only Ghostty and Nautilus
- GTK3 apps get traffic lights instead of plain buttons
- Yellow fades the window out before minimizing it

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Theme with colors, wallpapers, GTK styling and Hyprland animations, blur and shortcuts
- Menu bar, Dock, Spotlight, Launchpad, Mission Control, Control Center, App Switcher, hot corners and volume popup
- Working yellow and green traffic lights on Ghostty and Nautilus
- `install.sh` and `uninstall.sh`, including restoring your previous theme and bar
